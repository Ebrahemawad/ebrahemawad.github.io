01 / THE CONTEXT

The business question

Which suppliers contribute revenue and gross margin, and does revenue scale align with margin quality?

02 / THE BUILD

Project implementation

  1. Combined revenue, gross margin and margin-rate cards with monthly area charts.
  2. Used a supplier → product-group matrix with data bars to compare contributions.
  3. Added sales-team/category breakdowns and a MonthName slicer.

DASHBOARD DESIGN

A closer look at the report

01

Read revenue alongside margin

Revenue and gross margin cards make both measures visible together.

02

Compare supplier contributions

Data bars in the supplier and product-group matrix support comparisons.

03

Follow the monthly pattern

Monthly area charts and a MonthName slicer provide a view of trends.

03 / THE ANSWER

What the data shows

01

Two Brothers Mill contributes 5,124,316 in revenue: 52.9% of the displayed total.

02

Two Brothers Mill displays a 37% margin rate; Kappa Drinks displays 56% on revenue of 878,548.

03

Total margin divided by total revenue is 41.51%, rounded to 42% on the dashboard.

04 / THE NEXT STEP

Recommendations

  • Review supplier concentration together with pricing, discounts, costs and contract terms.
  • Label the secondary KPI cards with their individual Top 1 month context.
  • Clarify reporting year and currency and hide unfinished image-only pages before publication.

EVIDENCE & SCOPE

How to read these findings

Findings use the supplied MonthName = All snapshot. Secondary cards use separate Top 1 month filters; they are not verified prior-period comparisons. DAX, cost basis and reporting dates require native verification.

The dashboard image is a supplied snapshot. This website presents the case study; interactive analysis is performed in the native Excel or Power BI file.
