01 / THE CONTEXT

The business question

How do sales channels, product categories and delivery outcomes contribute to furniture-shop performance?

02 / THE BUILD

Project implementation

  1. Prepared a structured reporting table and used sales calculations to summarize performance.
  2. Built dashboard views with PivotTables, charts and governorate/customer-type slicers.
  3. Reviewed delivery classifications against promised and actual delivery dates.

03 / THE ANSWER

What the data shows

01

Homzmart contributes 65.0% of sales and 62.5% of prepared records.

02

Beds account for 63.8% of verified prepared-table sales.

03

Among 86 delivered records with both dates available, 42 were on time: 48.8%.

04 / THE NEXT STEP

Recommendations

  • Review the concentration of sales in Homzmart and bed products.
  • Reconcile the saved Best Seller pivot with the prepared source before refreshing the dashboard.
  • Treat missing delivery dates explicitly rather than classifying them as late.

EVIDENCE & SCOPE

How to read these findings

The saved Best Seller pivot differs from prepared-table totals. The saved delivery pivot uses a different denominator. Metrics here use checked source records; records are not confirmed distinct orders. The currency code is unverified.

The dashboard image is a supplied snapshot. This website presents the case study; interactive analysis is performed in the native Excel or Power BI file.
