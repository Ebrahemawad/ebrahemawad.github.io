01 / THE CONTEXT

The business question

Which products and transaction segments generate revenue, and how does performance vary across regions and sales representatives?

02 / THE BUILD

Approach & dashboard features

  1. Analyzed 499,999 transaction records from January 2014 to December 2016.
  2. Used NetSales = Units × Price × (1 − Discount) and checked every saved row calculation.
  3. Built summaries using seven PivotTables, five charts, three slicers and a date timeline.

03 / THE ANSWER

What the data shows

01

High-volume transactions (>280 units) are 19.0% of records and contribute 62.5% of net sales.

02

Carlota Doublers contributes 229.71M, or 21.0% of net sales.

03

The top nine products contribute 78.8% of sales; the top ten contribute 83.6%.

04

Alex has the highest regional revenue at 156.84M and Ismailia the lowest at 155.55M; the spread is narrow.

04 / THE NEXT STEP

Recommendations

  • Review service and discount policies for high-volume transactions.
  • Prioritize availability for leading products, while checking profitability before changing the mix.
  • Widen the Revenue card and replace the ARPU chart label with an accurate revenue/quantity title.

EVIDENCE & SCOPE

How to read these findings

All saved NetSales values matched recomputation within 0.001. Records are not verified distinct orders. Currency code, native slicer interactions and refresh remain unverified.

The dashboard image is a supplied snapshot. This website presents the case study; interactive analysis is performed in the native Excel or Power BI file.
